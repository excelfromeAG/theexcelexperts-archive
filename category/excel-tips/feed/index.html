]]> Excel is a powerful tool for organizing and analyzing data, but you need to start with a clear plan to use it effectively.

A good brief can help you achieve this. In this blog, we’ll cover what a brief is and what it should include to help you write a successful one for your Excel project.

What is a brief?

A brief is a concise summary of the key information about your project.

It outlines the goals, objectives, and requirements of the project, as well as the timeline, budget, and resources available. A good brief serves as a roadmap for the project and helps everyone involved to stay on track.

What to include in your brief?

  • Objectives: Start by clearly defining the goals and objectives of the project. What are you trying to achieve and why is this project important?
  • Scope: Outline the scope of the project, including what will and won’t be included. This will help to prevent scope creep and keep the project on track.
  • Requirements: Identify any specific requirements that need to be met, such as data sources, formatting, or calculations.
  • Timeline: Establish a timeline for the project, including key milestones and deadlines.
  • Budget: Determine the budget for the project, including any resources or expenses that need to be considered.
  • Resources: Identify the resources that will be needed for the project, including personnel, equipment, and technology.
  • Assumptions: List any assumptions that have been made about the project, including data availability and accuracy.
  • Risks: Identify any potential risks or challenges that could impact the project and plan how they will be addressed.
  • Deliverables: Specify what will be delivered at the end of the project, including reports, charts, or other data visualizations.

Conclusion

A good brief is an essential part of any successful Excel project.

It outlines the goals, objectives, and requirements of the project, as well as the timeline, budget, and resources available.

By following these guidelines, you can write a clear and effective brief that will help you to achieve your goals and ensure the success of your project.

]]> Cash flow is an important part of any business, and managing it properly is essential for a company’s success. One way you can manage your company’s cash flow is by creating a cash flow statement that will help you track your cash inflows and business expenses. 

You can then study this statement to identify any potential issues with your cash flow and also measure the company’s overall liquidity. 

This guide aims to provide tips on managing your cash flow and walk you through the steps of filling in a statement template.

Why is it Important for Businesses to Monitor Cash Flow? 

Cash flow is an important aspect of business because it is the movement of money in and out of the company. It directly impacts the company’s ability to meet its financial obligations and continue operations. 

Without positive cash flow, a company may struggle to pay bills, make payroll, or invest in growth opportunities. Therefore, keeping an eye on cash flow, and understanding how to manage it, as well as using tools to identify the cost of delays and other unexpected events, is crucial for the long-term success and sustainability of any business.

This is why monitoring your cash flow is key to the survival of your company, and you should get started on using a cash flow template as soon as possible.

Essential to Meet Financial Obligations

Each business will have some form of financial obligation. These are expenses that you have a responsibility to pay, such as rent for office spaces, debt repayments, or taxes. 

If you haven’t spent time monitoring your cash flow, you could end up in a position where you don’t have enough funds to cover these obligations, which could land you in trouble.

Help Business Owners Identify Potential Cash Shortages

Monitoring your cash flow can help you, as a business owner, identify and preempt cash shortages by tracking the inflow and outflow of funds, allowing for early identification of potential financial issues such as unexpected expenses or delays in receiving payments. 

This can help you make informed decisions about investments, expenses, and budgeting to ensure the stability and growth of your business.

Assess The Overall Financial Health of the Business

Spending time monitoring your cash flow can give you a better understanding of the overall health of your business because it allows you to spot patterns in financial performance and adjust your company’s spending accordingly. 

Can Help Identify Areas of Improvement 

Not only can monitoring your cash flow help with the things mentioned above, but it can also help you to spot potential areas of improvement. 

It can help you, as a business owner, make better-informed decisions about the future of your business and ensure that your finances remain stable.

Where Can You Find a Cash Flow Template?

One of the many benefits of Excel is that it allows business owners to quickly and easily create or use a cash flow statement template to help them to visualise the financial performance of the company. 

You can create custom Excel spreadsheets online as well as find templates that you can use to input your data. Filling in a cash flow template will involve the following steps.

Begin with the Balance Sheet

In Excel, the first step in creating a cash flow statement template is to enter the balance sheet information. This will include many different types of information relating to your accounts. 

The sort of data you may want to input here includes current accounts, accounts payable, accounts receivable, and other important financial data. 

This will serve as a foundation for your cash flow statement, and you will then input the rest of the data around this.

Identify Cash Inflows

Next, you will need to identify and enter the various sources of cash inflows. This can include any type of income, but most importantly, it should include continuous income streams that you know are always going to be coming into your business. 

This could be income from clients, projected sales, and anything else that will boost your overall funds. 

The data should all be as accurate as possible. Otherwise, this data is useless to you, so don’t edit the numbers to make things seem better than they are, as this doesn’t help you improve things.

Identify Cash Outflows

Next, you will need to identify and enter the various sources of cash outflows. This can be one of the more revealing steps when creating your cash flow statement. 

You should spend a lot of time going over every single expense that your business faces, from the major ones like office space rent and payroll to software subscriptions and equipment hire. 

The data here will give you a good idea of the expenses you can cut from your business to better balance your cash flow.

Calculate Net Cash Flow

Now, with all the information entered into your template, you can calculate the net cash flow for your business. 

Using Excel sums and subtracts functions, which are simple functions you can learn via an Excel training course, you can figure out exactly what your net cash flow is. 

This will involve subtracting your expenses from your income, which will be displayed in a net cash flow cell. You’re left with this income after paying all the financial obligations mentioned above.

Create Reports

With the help of your cash flow statement template, you can easily create Excel reports that will provide a snapshot of your business’s financial health at any given time. These reports can be invaluable to you as a business owner. They will allow you to easily spot any issues with spending, such as unexpected expenses or delays in receiving payments. 

By identifying these issues early on, you can take proactive measures to bring your cash flow back into a state you’re comfortable with and ensure the stability and growth of your business.

A comprehensive and accurate cash flow statement template is essential for businesses of all sizes. 

For a small business, income will likely be much lower, and budgeting will be far more important to ensure the business can make ends meet. And for larger, sprawling corporations, while income might be much higher, overheads will also be higher. 

As well as this, there is a much higher chance that a bigger company will have far more risk and volume of unnecessary expenses and will waste far more money than it needs to.

FAQs

Does Excel have a cash flow statement?

Excel does have some tools to help you create a cash flow statement. However, for those unfamiliar with using Excel and those unfamiliar with cash flow statements generally, they can be somewhat complicated to create.

How do I create a cash flow statement in Excel?

First, you should start by inputting all of your cash inflows, like your revenue, into one column on the spreadsheet and then all of your outflows in an opposite column. The outflows are your expenses. 

You then subtract the outflows from the inflows, and the resulting value will be your net cash flow for that time. You can then use formulas to generate an overall balance for your account.

How do you prepare a cash flow statement?

When preparing your cash flow statement, you must gather your company’s financial information, including cash inflows and outflows. 

You can then organise all of this information into a format, as demonstrated by a cash flow statement template which can then calculate and display useful data like cash flow.

What three things go on a statement of cash flows?

Usually, the three main things you’d put on a cash flow statement are general operation costs, investing costs, and financing activities. 

Operating costs involve sale income and payments to suppliers. Investing activities will involve the money you spend on investing in stocks, real estate, or other things in trading markets. 

And finally, financing activities involve any funds you receive from stocks, bonds, dividends, or money you pay to settle debts.

]]> If you need to create a project timeline, there are a few different ways you can do it. You could use a Gantt chart, which is a type of bar chart showing the start and end dates of each task in your project.

Or, you could use a spreadsheet program like Microsoft Excel to create a more traditional table-based timeline.

Whichever method you choose, the important thing is to make sure that your timeline is clear, concise, and easy to follow.

In this article, we’ll show you how to create a professional-looking project timeline in Excel.

What Is A Project Timeline?

A project timeline is a visual representation of the various tasks involved in a project and their respective deadlines.

It typically includes start and end dates for each task, including any dependencies (i.e., tasks that need to be completed before others can begin).

Project timelines are useful for individuals and teams alike, as they can help everyone involved in the project to stay on track.

Creating a timeline in Excel is a relatively straightforward process, and it’s a great way to make your project plan look more professional.

Steps To Creating A Project Timeline

Here are the steps to creating a project timeline in Excel:

Choose the right template

The first step in creating a timeline in Excel is to choose the most suitable template.

There are a few different timeline templates available in Excel, so you’ll need to decide which one is best for your project.

If you’re not sure which template to use, we recommend starting with the Basic Gantt Chart template.

To find this template, open Excel and go to File > New. Then, type “Gantt” into the search bar and select the Basic Gantt Chart template.

Add your tasks

Once you’ve selected a template, it’s time to start adding your tasks. 

To do this, simply enter the task name and duration into the appropriate columns.

If you’re using the Basic Gantt Chart template, you’ll need to enter your tasks in the “Task Name” column and the duration in the “Duration” column.

You can also add start and end dates by clicking on the “Add Start Date” and “Add End Date” buttons.

If you want to add more details about each task, you can do so in the “notes” section.

This is optional, but it can be helpful to include additional information, such as who is responsible for each task, what the task entails, etc.

Add any dependencies

Once you’ve added all your tasks, it’s time to add any dependencies.

To do this, simply click on the “Predecessors” column and enter the task that needs to be completed before the current task can begin.

For example, if task B can’t start until task A is finished, you would enter “A” in the “Predecessors” column for task B.

You can also add multiple dependencies by separating them with a comma.

For example, if task C can’t start until both tasks A and B are finished, you would enter “A,B” in the “Predecessors” column for task C.

Add milestones (optional)

If your project has any milestones, you can add them to your timeline by clicking on the “Milestones” tab and entering the milestone name and date.

This is optional, but it can be helpful to include milestones as they can give you a better overview of your project’s progress.

Format your timeline

Once you’ve added all of your tasks and dependencies, it’s time to format your timeline.

To do this, simply select the cells you want to format and then click on the “Format” tab.

You can then change the colour, font, and other aspects of your timeline.

You can also use the “Conditional Formatting” feature to highlight tasks that are behind schedule or at risk of not being completed on time.

To do this, simply select the cells you want to format and then click on the “Conditional Formatting” button.

From here, you can choose from a variety of different options, such as “Highlight Cell Rules” and “Data Bars”.

Export your timeline

Once you’re happy with your timeline, you can export it by going to File > Export.

From here, you can choose to export your timeline as an image or PDF file.

You can also choose to save your timeline as an Excel file so you can edit it later if necessary.

Final Thoughts: How A Project Timeline Can Help You

A project timeline is a great way to keep track of your project’s progress and ensure it is on track and remains that way.

It can also help you identify any potential problems that could impact the project’s completion date.

If you’re not sure how to create a timeline, we recommend using one of the templates mentioned above.

Once you’ve created your timeline, be sure to export it so you can keep a copy for yourself and share it with others involved in the project.

How To Lock Cells In Excel

Locking cells in Excel is very important if you are sharing your spreadsheet, which is a common requirement in most businesses. It can also come in handy if you are a beginner and you want to avoid losing any progress that you have already made. 

Let’s start by talking you through how to lock an entire spreadsheet. When you protect a spreadsheet, all of the cells will be locked, and no one will be able to edit them.

Find the review tab at the top of the screen. In the dropdown, click Protect Sheet. A pop-up will prompt you to set a password and offer you a list of actions that you can allow for users.

Locking and unlocking specific cells is simple. Select the cells that you want to lock, right click, and open the Format Cells window.

If you want to prevent other users from editing cells that have specific controls and formulas, then you will need to lock formatting. Follow the same steps as above and clear the Locked checkbox.

Go to the Home tab and find Go To Special. Click Formulas, and then click OK to select every formula in the spreadsheet. Now, return to the Protection tab and click Locked again. Now, the formatting and formulas cannot be changed. 

Locked Check Box

You may want to lock any checkboxes on your spreadsheet. There are two cells to protect here. First, you must protect the cell with the check box. Second, you must protect the cell that this check box is linked to.

Select both cells, and then find the Format Cells window. Open the Protection tab and click Locked. This will mean that the status of the check box in the cell cannot be altered. This is very important if you are sending a sheet with survey results, for example.

The Protection Tab

The protection tab in Excel offers you different options depending on how completely you want to protect your project. If you choose Protect Workbook, the entire file will be protected if you click Protect Structure.

If you click Protect Sheet, you will be given many more options for allowing users access. By default, it will allow users without a password to select cells. You can choose to grant access for tasks such as inserting rows and links, deleting rows and columns, and formatting.

Remember that colours count as formatting, so select the cells in question and find the Protection tab. Here, you will see a list of actions you can allow users to do. Make sure that the format cells box is not checked and hit OK.

Shared Excel Sheets
