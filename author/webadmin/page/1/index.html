How To Use Data Analysis To Improve Marketing Strategies For Small Businesses

Posted on April 5, 2023 by Ben Hiron-Grimes - Blog, Excel For Business

Marketing is one of the most critical aspects of any business. However, small businesses often face the challenge of limited resources, which makes it challenging to compete with larger companies.

Data analysis is a powerful tool that can help small businesses improve their marketing strategies and achieve their goals. In this blog, we will explore how small businesses can use data analysis to improve their marketing strategies and grow their customer base.

1. Define your goals

Before you start analysing data, it’s essential to define your marketing goals. What do you want to achieve with your marketing efforts? Do you want to increase brand awareness, generate more leads, or drive sales?

Defining your goals will help you focus your analysis and ensure that you are collecting the right data.

2. Collect and analyse customer data

Customer data is a goldmine of information that can help small businesses improve their marketing strategies. By collecting and analysing customer data, small businesses can identify customer preferences, buying habits, and pain points.

This allows them to tailor their marketing efforts to meet customer needs and improve their customer experience.

There are several ways to collect customer data, including surveys, social media monitoring, and website analytics.

Once you have collected the data, you can use tools like Excel or Google Sheets to analyse it. Look for patterns and trends in the data to identify areas where you can improve your marketing efforts.

3. Analyse marketing data

Marketing data is another valuable source of information that can help small businesses improve their marketing strategies.

By analysing marketing data, small businesses can determine which campaigns are most effective and which channels are generating the most leads. This allows them to allocate their marketing budget more effectively and improve their return on investment.

Marketing data includes metrics such as website traffic, email open rates, click-through rates, conversion rates, and social media engagement.

Analyse this data to identify trends and patterns and adjust your marketing strategies accordingly.

4. Analyse competitor data

Analysing competitor data is another essential part of data analysis for small businesses. By analysing competitor data, small businesses can identify their strengths and weaknesses and adjust their strategies accordingly.

This allows them to differentiate themselves from their competitors and position themselves for success.

Competitor data includes metrics such as website traffic, social media engagement, and search engine rankings. Use tools like Google Analytics and SEMrush to analyse competitor data and identify areas where you can improve your marketing strategies.

5. Use data visualization tools

Data visualization tools are an essential part of data analysis for small businesses. They allow you to turn raw data into easy-to-understand visuals that can help you identify patterns and trends quickly.

Use tools like Tableau, Google Data Studio, and Microsoft Power BI to create visualizations of your data.

Visualizations can include charts, graphs, and heat maps that allow you to see data patterns at a glance. Use these visualizations to identify areas where you can improve your marketing strategies and communicate your findings to your team.

Conclusion

Data analysis is a powerful tool that can help small businesses improve their marketing strategies and achieve their goals.

By collecting and analysing customer data, marketing data, and competitor data, small businesses can identify trends and patterns and adjust their strategies accordingly. Use data visualization tools to communicate your findings to your team and make informed decisions about your marketing efforts.

With the right data analysis tools and strategies, small businesses can compete with larger companies and grow their customer base.

How to Write a Good Brief for Your Excel Project

Posted on February 28, 2023 by Ben Hiron-Grimes - Excel Tips

Excel is a powerful tool for organizing and analyzing data, but you need to start with a clear plan to use it effectively.

A good brief can help you achieve this. In this blog, we’ll cover what a brief is and what it should include to help you write a successful one for your Excel project.

What is a brief?

A brief is a concise summary of the key information about your project.

It outlines the goals, objectives, and requirements of the project, as well as the timeline, budget, and resources available. A good brief serves as a roadmap for the project and helps everyone involved to stay on track.

What to include in your brief?

  • Objectives: Start by clearly defining the goals and objectives of the project. What are you trying to achieve and why is this project important?
  • Scope: Outline the scope of the project, including what will and won’t be included. This will help to prevent scope creep and keep the project on track.
  • Requirements: Identify any specific requirements that need to be met, such as data sources, formatting, or calculations.
  • Timeline: Establish a timeline for the project, including key milestones and deadlines.
  • Budget: Determine the budget for the project, including any resources or expenses that need to be considered.
  • Resources: Identify the resources that will be needed for the project, including personnel, equipment, and technology.
  • Assumptions: List any assumptions that have been made about the project, including data availability and accuracy.
  • Risks: Identify any potential risks or challenges that could impact the project and plan how they will be addressed.
  • Deliverables: Specify what will be delivered at the end of the project, including reports, charts, or other data visualizations.

Conclusion

A good brief is an essential part of any successful Excel project.

It outlines the goals, objectives, and requirements of the project, as well as the timeline, budget, and resources available.

By following these guidelines, you can write a clear and effective brief that will help you to achieve your goals and ensure the success of your project.

What is Excel VBA?

Excel VBA is short for Visual Basic for Applications. The easiest way to explain VBA is that it is the language that Microsoft that allows you to tell the programme what to do.

You do this by creating macros (or macroinstructions) which are essentially instructions for Excel. These formulas are characters that fulfil a function when you input them into your sheet. 

VBA can be used across the full range of Microsoft Office programmes, but it can be particularly useful in Excel. In short, VBA gives you the power to make your own solutions and alter your sheets as you want.

VBA Editor or VB Editor?

VBA editor (or VB editor) is a separate programme from Excel, although it is part of it. You will need to download VB Editor if you want to use VBA.

Every time you create and save a macro, it will be stored in VB Editor, or you can create one directly in there if you want. You can edit it any time you want, and it is kept separate from your worksheet so only you have access to it.

Does VBA Work on Macs?

The short answer to this question is “yes, depending on what you’re using.” Macs come with their own version of Excel, which is called Numbers.

In many ways, Numbers is similar to Excel, but macros don’t work on it. Therefore, if you don’t have Excel, you can’t use Macros.

This is because VBA works on Microsoft office suite products. However, if you have Microsoft Excel on your Mac, then VBA should work perfectly fine. 

How to Get VBA on Your Mac

If your Mac has Excel, then you will be able to get VBA and start creating Macros to handle your tasks, automate functions and start handling large quantities of data.

Here are the steps that you will need to take. 

  1. Download Microsoft Excel onto your Mac.
  2. Find the Developer tab by clicking Preferences on the Excel menu.
  3. Click the Ribbon in Sharing and Privacy and find Developer in the Customize section.
  4. Check that you have the Developer tab on your toolbar and click on it.
  5. Find the Visual Basic group on the Developer tab and click Editor.

Clicking on the Editor section will open up the Microsoft Visual Basic editor, and this is where you can get started entering and editing code. It will be blank at first, so don’t worry!

How to Use VBA on Macs

Macros are at the core of VBA regardless of whether you are using a Mac, a Microsoft machine, or any other computer.

The good news is that it is working on a Mac and does not make any difference when it comes to creating, enabling, or using macros.

The first thing to do once you have found the Developer tab and have followed the step above is to ensure that you have enabled macros to allow them to run in Excel. Find the Security and Privacy section in Excel by heading to Preferences.

Here, you can choose to allow specific macros or all of the ones that Excel suggests. However, remember that if you allow all, then there may be some that you do not want. Doing this will also allow you to start creating your own macros.

You can find the Record Macro button on the Developer tab, where you will find instructions on how to make one.

Why Should You Use VBA?

Using VBA is a great way to take your workbooks from something that is purely functional to something that works for you. There are many specific reasons why it could be great for your business. Here are three that are true for everyone.

  1. It is easier than you might realise, especially once you get the hang of it.
  2. It will save you time by automating time-consuming tasks that can be a massive drain.
  3. You can personalise your Excel and Office experience by creating the macros you need.

It is also important to remember that using and creating macros is free, so if you find that one is not working for you, you can simply toss it out and try to create your own that does.

Excel Spreadsheet Help: A Beginner’s Guide to Data Organisation

Posted on February 8, 2023 by Ben Hiron-Grimes - Excel

If you have never used Microsoft Excel before, it can all seem a little overwhelming. There are so many different tools and functions at your fingertips.

But with a little research and practice, you will be amazed by what you can accomplish with an Excel spreadsheet. In this article, we are going to break down everything that you need to know to start your first Excel spreadsheet and get organising that data. Let’s get started!

Getting Started With Your Excel Spreadsheet: Microsoft Excel Basics

The best place to start with Excel spreadsheet help is at the beginning. Don’t worry, you will get your bearings in no time. When you first open the programme, you will see a new blank worksheet.

Your worksheet is made up of a grid of cells. These cells are where you will input data, and they are aligned by vertical columns (labelled by letter) and horizontal rows (labelled by number). 

  1. Each cell is referred to by its column and row number, so the third cell along the top row will be cell C1.
  2. You can type your data directly into the cell, or into the taskbar that appears when you click on the cell.
  3. You can divide your workbook into different worksheets, and their tabs are at the bottom of the screen.
  4. When you have finished entering data into a cell, press ENTER or TAB to move to the next one.
  5. You can save your workbook by clicking Save, or Save As.

Once you have inputted the data that you want, you can start organising it. 

How to Organise Your Data using an Excel Spreadsheet

One of the simplest and most effective tools that Excel offers is organising data to make it more comprehensible, solving sums, and more.

There are a lot of different options for organising your data, so choose the one that works best for you. Here are the key steps to help you get started with your first workbook.

  1. Check if there’s a helpful ready-made template by clicking File then New.
  2. You can highlight key data by filling the cells with colour.
  3. Sort and filter data by alphabetical order or size by selecting the cells that you want to sort.
  4. Filter out cells that you don’t want to include by clicking the Sort and Filter button. 
  5. Tidy it up by using Wrap Text to ensure that all text is visible.

Once you are more confident, you can start using formulas to add up the totals of the cells in your sheet, add a number format, or create a graph or chart to represent your data.

Benefits of Using an Excel Spreadsheet to Organise Your Data

Excel allows people to get to grips with all kinds of data, whether you are looking to store addresses for a mailing list or figure out where your money is going from your budget. 

Time-Saving Automation

You can also use macros, which are commands that are entered into cells to perform tasks, to save even more time.

Easy Accessibility

Create workbooks that you can share with your colleagues or clients to either show them what you are working on or invite them to work on or edit themselves.

Excel also allows its users to lock cells or spreadsheets if they do not want anything to be changed.

You can get to grips with Excel quickly. You don’t need to be an accountant to create a detailed budget, and you don’t need to be a programmer to use formulas. 

Your confidence will grow by trying out the wide range of tools that you can quickly learn how to use.

Common Excel Errors and How to Avoid Them

Excel will alert you to errors. There are several reasons why Excel may display an error message, but the specific cause will generally be pointed out by the message itself. 

Here are some of the most common Excel errors and how to avoid them.

#VALUE!

This will appear because one or more of the cells will contain a value that does not match the formula, like a rogue cell containing letters instead of numbers.

You can avoid this by double-checking the cells that you have included.

#DIV/0!

This error occurs when you are asking Excel to divide the value of two cells, but one of them is either blank or has a value of zero. You may have included the wrong cell or not added the data yet.

To avoid this, make sure you have added or checked the number value into the second cell.

######

This means that you have entered a number into a cell that is too big to display. That number hasn’t gone anywhere, and you can see it by widening the width of the column that the cell is in.

To avoid this error, give yourself enough room in the cells to work in.

#NAME?

Do not be too alarmed if the #NAME? error appears when entering an equation. Excel does not understand the formula’s text, and it may be due to a typo.

To avoid this error, double-check that you have written the command correctly.

If you are doing data analysis but choose not to use Excel models or other similar tools, you might find that this will be much more difficult to do.

The Ultimate Guide to Using a Cash Flow Statement Template

Posted on January 20, 2023 by Ben Hiron-Grimes - Excel Tips

Cash flow is an important part of any business, and managing it properly is essential for a company’s success. One way you can manage your company’s cash flow is by creating a cash flow statement that will help you track your cash inflows and business expenses. 

You can then study this statement to identify any potential issues with your cash flow and also measure the company’s overall liquidity. 

This guide aims to provide tips on managing your cash flow and walk you through the steps of filling in a statement template.

Why is it Important for Businesses to Monitor Cash Flow? 

Cash flow is an important aspect of business because it is the movement of money in and out of the company. It directly impacts the company’s ability to meet its financial obligations and continue operations. 

Without positive cash flow, a company may struggle to pay bills, make payroll, or invest in growth opportunities. Therefore, keeping an eye on cash flow, and understanding how to manage it, as well as using tools to identify the cost of delays and other unexpected events, is crucial for the long-term success and sustainability of any business.

This is why monitoring your cash flow is key to the survival of your company, and you should get started on using a cash flow template as soon as possible.

Essential to Meet Financial Obligations

Each business will have some form of financial obligation. These are expenses that you have a responsibility to pay, such as rent for office spaces, debt repayments, or taxes. 

If you haven’t spent time monitoring your cash flow, you could end up in a position where you don’t have enough funds to cover these obligations, which could land you in trouble.

Help Business Owners Identify Potential Cash Shortages

Monitoring your cash flow can help you, as a business owner, identify and preempt cash shortages by tracking the inflow and outflow of funds, allowing for early identification of potential financial issues such as unexpected expenses or delays in receiving payments. 

This can help you make informed decisions about investments, expenses, and budgeting to ensure the stability and growth of your business.

Assess The Overall Financial Health of the Business

Spending time monitoring your cash flow can give you a better understanding of the overall health of your business because it allows you to spot patterns in financial performance and adjust your company’s spending accordingly. 

Can Help Identify Areas of Improvement 

Not only can monitoring your cash flow help with the things mentioned above, but it can also help you to spot potential areas of improvement. 

It can help you, as a business owner, make better-informed decisions about the future of your business and ensure that your finances remain stable.

Where Can You Find a Cash Flow Template?

One of the many benefits of Excel is that it allows business owners to quickly and easily create or use a cash flow statement template to help them to visualise the financial performance of the company. 

You can create custom Excel spreadsheets online as well as find templates that you can use to input your data. Filling in a cash flow template will involve the following steps.

Begin with the Balance Sheet

In Excel, the first step in creating a cash flow statement template is to enter the balance sheet information. This will include many different types of information relating to your accounts. 

The sort of data you may want to input here includes current accounts, accounts payable, accounts receivable, and other important financial data. 

This will serve as a foundation for your cash flow statement, and you will then input the rest of the data around this.

Identify Cash Inflows

Next, you will need to identify and enter the various sources of cash inflows. This can include any type of income, but most importantly, it should include continuous income streams that you know are always going to be coming into your business. 

This could be income from clients, projected sales, and anything else that will boost your overall funds. 

The data should all be as accurate as possible. Otherwise, this data is useless to you, so don’t edit the numbers to make things seem better than they are, as this doesn’t help you improve things.

Identify Cash Outflows

Next, you will need to identify and enter the various sources of cash outflows. This can be one of the more revealing steps when creating your cash flow statement. 

You should spend a lot of time going over every single expense that your business faces, from the major ones like office space rent and payroll to software subscriptions and equipment hire. 

The data here will give you a good idea of the expenses you can cut from your business to better balance your cash flow.

Calculate Net Cash Flow

Now, with all the information entered into your template, you can calculate the net cash flow for your business. 

Using Excel sums and subtracts functions, which are simple functions you can learn via an Excel training course, you can figure out exactly what your net cash flow is. 

This will involve subtracting your expenses from your income, which will be displayed in a net cash flow cell. You’re left with this income after paying all the financial obligations mentioned above.

Create Reports

With the help of your cash flow statement template, you can easily create Excel reports that will provide a snapshot of your business’s financial health at any given time. These reports can be invaluable to you as a business owner. They will allow you to easily spot any issues with spending, such as unexpected expenses or delays in receiving payments. 

By identifying these issues early on, you can take proactive measures to bring your cash flow back into a state you’re comfortable with and ensure the stability and growth of your business.

FAQs

Does Excel have a cash flow statement?

Excel does have some tools to help you create a cash flow statement. However, for those unfamiliar with using Excel and those unfamiliar with cash flow statements generally, they can be somewhat complicated to create.

How do I create a cash flow statement in Excel?

First, you should start by inputting all of your cash inflows, like your revenue, into one column on the spreadsheet and then all of your outflows in an opposite column. The outflows are your expenses. 

You then subtract the outflows from the inflows, and the resulting value will be your net cash flow for that time. You can then use formulas to generate an overall balance for your account.

How do you prepare a cash flow statement?

When preparing your cash flow statement, you must gather your company’s financial information, including cash inflows and outflows. 

You can then organise all of this information into a format, as demonstrated by a cash flow statement template which can then calculate and display useful data like cash flow.

What three things go on a statement of cash flows?

Usually, the three main things you’d put on a cash flow statement are general operation costs, investing costs, and financing activities. 

Operating costs involve sale income and payments to suppliers. Investing activities will involve the money you spend on investing in stocks, real estate, or other things in trading markets. 

And finally, financing activities involve any funds you receive from stocks, bonds, dividends, or money you pay to settle debts.

In this blog post, The Excel Experts explain how you can make the most of Excel’s document recovery solutions to save your work if disaster strikes. 

Why Recover Your Excel Documents?

There are many reasons why you might need to recover your Excel documents. For example, if your computer ran out of battery or shut down unexpectedly while you were editing a workbook, and you hadn’t saved your changes.

Alternatively, if you accidentally delete important information, then you can use the recovery tool to restore the Excel document to its previous state and get the data back. 

Whatever the reason, accidents happen, and even the most vigilant of business leaders and team members make mistakes and lose data from time to time.

With the help of Microsoft Excel’s recovery solution, these errors don’t have to be disastrous and can easily be rectified. 

How To Recover Unsaved Excel Files in Windows

Recovering unsaved files on a laptop or computer that operates using Windows is quick and easy. This simple step-by-step guide should help you to manage the process efficiently and get your files back in no time.

Whether your computer shuts down by itself or the program crashes, you can quickly get your work back and save it. 

Step One

The first step is to reopen Excel and go to the file section. You’ll then need to go to the Open tab, which is usually near the New one. You should then see all your recent workbooks. If the program closed quickly, then you might be able to see the recovery section there. 

Step Two

Alternatively, at the bottom left you’ll see a small, white square that says Recover Unsaved Workbooks and has a picture of an open file next to it. Click on this button. The Recover Unsaved Workbooks button will open a new window into the desktop folder where Excel saves all recovered, unsaved documents. 

Step Three

In this folder, you’ll see all the unsaved documents that Excel has kept for you. These documents might not have titles, but you can tell them apart by reviewing the date they were last modified or by opening them.

Once you open an unsaved file and identify it as the one you need, you can save it in a prominent location under a file name that you will recognise. 

How To Recover Unsaved Excel Files on a Mac

Apple Macs are different to devices that use Windows, as they use Apple’s own operating system. While you can still run Microsoft programs on these devices, they operate and appear differently. Therefore, the steps you need to take when recovering unsaved Excel files on a Mac aren’t the same as the ones you take when using a Windows computer. 

Step One

There are several methods that you can use to recover unsaved Excel files on a Mac, but the simplest one is to open Excel and click on the file dropdown menu. Under Open there will be an option that is called Open Recent, with a small arrow next to it. 

Step Two

If you hover your mouse over the Open Recent option, then to the left will be a list of recently used Excel documents, including any that were unsaved when the program shut down unexpectedly. If you see the document you need, then you can open it and then save it.  

Step Three

At the bottom of the list will be the More option, which you can open if you can’t find the file in the list. You’ll then be taken to the desktop destination where your recent Excel files are held and you can review them and find the one you need.

You can also check the temporary folder on your desktop to see if the document can be found there. 

How To Recover Overwritten Excel Files

If you overwrite an Excel workbook and then need to restore the previous version, you can do so by going to File- Info- Manage Workbook. 

In this box, you will see a dropdown arrow, which will allow you to see older versions of the document. The dropdown will show recent versions of the document. If you don’t see the right option in the dropdown menu, you can then click on Recover Unsaved Workbooks. 

This will open the Microsoft folder on your computer where unsaved or older versions of Excel documents are saved. You can then restore the older version of the Excel file and get your information back. 

5 Excel File Saving Tips

To reduce your chances of losing work and save your precious time, you need to understand how to make the most of Excel and save your documents correctly.

Here are some of our expert tips on how to save Excel files and help you to maximise your productivity when using the program. 

1. Save Without Losing Formatting

If you find that you keep losing your formatting when saving Excel documents, then click Save As and use the dropdown to suggest a different type of workbook.

This might be a binary or a macro-enabled workbook. A .xlsx workbook should keep all your formatting saved, so this is often the most popular option, but if it keeps changing, you might need to consider a different type. 

2. Saving An Excel File As CSV or XLSX

To save an Excel file as a different type, you go to Save As and choose the option you want. A CSV or Comma delimited workbook is more compatible with other programs than the XLSX version, so this could be a useful solution for anyone who wants to integrate Excel with other tools. 

3. Saving A File After Editing

After editing an Excel document, you should save it to ensure that your changes are safe. You can do this by clicking the save button at the top of the file.

If you want to make a new version of the document after each set of edits, you can choose Save As and then rename the file to differentiate it from older versions. 

4. Cleaning Up Your Excel Workbook For Less Memory

If you have a large Excel file that’s using up a lot of memory, then you can try to get rid of old data or clean it up by removing unused macros or other formatting solutions that could be contributing to its size.

Consider storing unused data in a different file or getting rid of it to make the file smaller and reduce the space it takes up on your computer or Cloud storage solution. 

5. Reducing Your Excel File Size

If you’ve reduced the data in your workbook, but the size is still large, then you can reduce the size of the document further.

There are several techniques you can use, the most notable being to compress the file. You can do this by going to the file’s saved location, left-clicking on the document name and going to the send to, then go to the compressed (zipped) folder.

You can then make a compressed version of the file, which you can be great for saving memory and sending it via email.
